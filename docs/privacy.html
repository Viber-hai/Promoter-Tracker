Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

This policy explains how the Promoter Tracker Android app handles information entered into the app and information used by its optional backup features. The app is designed primarily for personal, on-device sales and incentive tracking.

1. Information you enter

Product names, prices or RRPs, quantities, targets, incentive values and app preferences are stored locally on your device by the app. The project does not operate a Promoter Tracker account service or its own cloud database for this information.

2. Device security

The Android app can use your device's biometric authentication and an app PIN, where configured, to control access to protected app features. Biometric matching is handled by Android. The app receives the authentication result; it does not receive your fingerprint image or biometric template.

3. Local backups

The Android app provides local backup and restore options. Backup files created by the native Android app use the app's encryption process. Keep your backup files and any recovery password safe. If you lose the recovery password for a password-protected backup, you may not be able to restore it.

Browser-based previews or other non-native environments may offer different backup formats and protections. Check the in-app backup screen and file type before moving a backup between environments.

4. Optional Google Drive backups

If you choose to connect a Google account and enable or request Google Drive backups, the app uses Google's sign-in and Drive services to store and retrieve backup files in the app-specific Drive data area. The app requests the Drive app-data scope; it is not designed to browse your ordinary Drive files.

Backup content is encrypted by the app before upload. Google processes the account connection and stores the uploaded encrypted file under its own privacy and security terms. Your Google account email may be shown in the app to identify the connected account. You can disconnect the account from the app's backup settings.

Google's handling of account information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Sharing and disclosure

The project does not sell the sales or incentive information you enter. Information may be transferred to Google when you choose to use Google Drive backup, and may be shared by you through any export, file-sharing or device feature you choose to use.

6. Retention and deletion

Information stored locally remains on your device until you remove it, clear the app's data, uninstall the app, or replace it through a restore/reset action. Backup files saved to your device or Google Drive may remain there until you delete them using the relevant device or Google Drive controls.

7. Children's privacy

Promoter Tracker is a productivity tool and is not designed to collect personal information from children through an online account service.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when a new version is published.

9. Contact and support

For questions about the project or to report a privacy concern, open an issue in the Promoter Tracker GitHub repository. Do not include passwords, recovery phrases, signing credentials or private backup files in a public issue.
