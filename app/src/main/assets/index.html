What’s new

Updates in this version of Promoter Tracker.

Google Drive backups
  • Connect your Google account to keep encrypted backups in Drive.
  • Save a backup to Drive whenever you want, or restore a previous backup.
  • Choose Local or Google Drive as the single automatic backup destination.
  • Set separate schedules and storage options for each destination.
Incentive privacy
  • Incentives stay blurred until you choose to reveal them.
  • Unlock incentive amounts securely with your fingerprint.
  • Turn fingerprint verification for incentives on or off in Settings.
  • Tap a revealed incentive again to hide it.
App security
  • Protect the app with fingerprint unlock and a six-digit backup PIN.
  • Choose when the app locks after inactivity.
Backup & restore
  • Keep your manual and automatic backups encrypted.
  • Restore encrypted backups on the same phone without entering a password.
  • Set a recovery password to restore backups on a new phone.
  • Choose backup folders and automatic backup schedules.
  • Import older JSON backups when moving to encrypted backups.
Sales & incentives
  • Edit monthly Handset & Tablet and Audio & Wearables targets in Settings.
  • View sales progress, category performance, and your total incentive.
  • Calculate incentives using the full entered per-unit amount below 80% achievement and the weighted incentive formula from 80% onward.
  • Update model quantities and rearrange products by pressing and holding a card.
Everyday improvements
  • Keep the main screen focused on product cards, with management tools in the menu.
  • Use the app in light or dark mode with clearer backup and settings text.
  • See a red indicator when there are new features to explore.
